Research phases

Each phase shows only its purpose, status and main output. Detailed protocols belong inside the linked phase pages, where they can misbehave without crowding the homepage.

P0Active

Feedstock Characterisation

Standardise OP and establish reliable physical, chemical and thermal baselines.

Output: accepted baseline

P1Active

Pretreatment Screening

Compare chemical, washing and mineralisation routes for improved surface compatibility.

Output: top treatment routes

P2Active

Thermal and Ash Route

Classify thermally treated OP and ash as reactive, inert, aggregate-like or unsuitable.

Output: ash role decision

P3Next

Mortar Screening

Compare untreated, treated and ash routes under controlled mortar conditions.

Output: route selection

P4Planned

Concrete Optimisation

Optimise the strongest route and validate ITZ, strength and thermal trade-offs.

Output: operating mix window

P5Conditional

Durability Validation

Test moisture transport, cyclic exposure, shrinkage and long-term stability.

Output: durability boundary

P6Extension

Hybrid and Cross-Matrix

Assess hybrid ash-particulate, EICP/MICP and selected polymer routes.

Output: mechanism extension

P7Final

Synthesis and LCA

Integrate performance, sustainability, application limits and publication outputs.

Output: engineering framework
